About Me

Background, engineering foundation, and web development work

Who Am I?

Hello! I'm Fadipe Ayomide John, a web developer and Computer Engineering graduate (Distinction, 3.54/4.00 CGPA) from the Federal Polytechnic, Ilaro. I build clean, responsive websites and personal/professional portfolios that help individuals, businesses, and organizations establish a credible online presence.

During my time at the Federal Polytechnic, Ilaro, I participated in the Annual Interdepartmental Quiz Competition organized by the National Association of Polytechnic Engineering Students (NAPES) and received an Award of Recognition from the National Association of Computer Engineering Students (NACOMES) for serving as an ND Students Tutor during the 2024/2025 academic session. I also received a Certificate of Academic Excellence from the School of Virtue.

Alongside my technical work, I have gained hands-on operational leadership experience serving as a Supervisor at Mega Chicken Restaurant, coordinating daily operations, supporting team members, and maintaining high service standards. That combination of structured engineering thinking and real-world accountability shapes how I scope, build, and deliver websites.
